Here's some of the things we've added to basic IRC protocol to support our needs.
DCC AUDIO
---------
Here we send a message with the IP and port to connect for RTP media:
	DCC AUDIO host port-ear port-mouth session-key

	The server will advertise a port pair for two-way audio
	Ear and mouth are relative to the CLIENT meaning they LISTEN on the
	EAR port and TRANSMIT on the MOUTH port.
